## 二、标题结构公式

### 标准公式

```
[Brand] + [核心产品词] + [with/and + 核心功能] + [附加功能] + [for 场景] + (颜色)
```

### 示例拆解

```
GTPLAYER Gaming Chair, Computer Chair with Footrest and Lumbar Support,
Height Adjustable Game Chair with 360°-Swivel Seat and Headrest and for 
Office or Gaming (Pearl White)

├─ Brand: GTPLAYER
├─ 核心: Gaming Chair / Computer Chair
├─ 核心功能: with Footrest and Lumbar Support
├─ 附加功能: Height Adjustable / 360°-Swivel Seat / Headrest
├─ 场景: for Office or Gaming
└─ 颜色: Pearl White
```

## 四、KEIKI电竞椅Listing优化建议

### 标题公式

```
KEIKI Gaming Chair, [材质] [款式] Computer Chair with 
Footrest and [功能], Ergonomic Office Chair with Lumbar 
Support and Headrest for Adults, [颜色]
```

### 五点建议结构

1. **核心舒适度** - 强调人体工学、腰部支撑
2. **材质工艺** - Leather/Fabric + 高密度海绵
3. **功能亮点** - 可调节、脚踏板、按摩腰枕
4. **耐用性** - 承重、金属底座、认证
5. **售后保障** - 安装简单、质保服务

### 关键词埋词优先级

**必埋词**: gaming chair, computer chair, ergonomic, footrest, lumbar support, headrest, adjustable, office chair

**高价值词**: massage lumbar, 360 swivel, reclining, big and tall, heavy duty, adults, racing style, pocket spring

**差异化词**: FSC-certified, breathable fabric, premium leather, linkage armrests
