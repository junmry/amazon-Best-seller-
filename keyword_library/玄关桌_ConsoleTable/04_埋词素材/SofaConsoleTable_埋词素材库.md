## 三、卖点表述素材

### 3.1 窄型设计表述（类目核心卖点）

**尺寸强调：**
- Ultra-slim **5.9 inch** depth fits tight spaces
- Skinny **console table** only **X inches** wide
- Narrow design slides perfectly **behind couch**
- Space-saving silhouette for **small apartments**
- Minimal footprint, maximum functionality

**空间解决方案：**
- Ideal for **narrow hallways** and **tight entryways**
- Fits where bulkier furniture cannot
- Perfect **behind-the-sofa** storage without overwhelming the room
- Makes use of **dead space behind couch**

### 3.2 长度/尺寸可变表述

**长度强调：**
- Extra-long **78 inch** / **102 inch** spans wide sofas
- **Adjustable length** from **86.6 to 110.2 inches**
- Stretches the full length of your **sectional**
- Long surface for lamps, décor, and daily essentials

**高度强调：**
- **Height adjustable** from **23.9 to 34.4 inches**
- Customize to match your **sofa back height**
- Adjustable legs for uneven floors

### 3.3 电源功能表述

**充电功能：**
- Built-in **2 power outlets** and **USB-C ports**
- **Charging station** conveniently located **behind couch**
- Power your devices without visible cords
- Integrated outlet for lamps and electronics
- USB ports for phones and tablets

**安全性：**
- ETL listed for safety
- Surge protection built-in
- Cord management keeps wires tidy

### 3.4 储物功能表述

**开放式储物：**
- **2-tier** open shelving for baskets and books
- Lower shelf keeps essentials within reach
- Display décor on top, store items below
- Open design for easy access

**封闭式储物：**
- **Drawer** conceals clutter
- Closed storage hides remote controls and cables
- Mix of open and closed storage options

### 3.5 材质工艺表述

**实木款：**
- Solid wood construction built to last
- Natural grain pattern unique to each piece
- Durable hardwood withstands daily use
- Premium oak/walnut finish

**金属/工业风：**
- Powder-coated steel frame resists rust
- Industrial pipe design adds character
- Sturdy metal base supports heavy items
- Mix of wood top and metal frame

** farmhouse风：**
- Rustic farmhouse style warms any space
- Distressed wood finish for vintage charm
- X-frame side design classic farmhouse detail
