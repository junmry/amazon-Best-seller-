### 4.3 Substitution — 替代防御词（拦截"不用你也能解决"）

**防御目标 → 埋词短语：**
- 替代半导体酒柜 → `compressor cooling wine refrigerator (not thermoelectric)`
- 替代小冰箱 → `large capacity wine fridge (not mini fridge)`
- 替代冰桶/传统储酒 → `electric wine cooler (no ice needed)`
- 替代户外冰箱 → `indoor wine cellar (not outdoor refrigerator)`
- 替代商用冷柜 → `home wine refrigerator (not commercial cooler)`
- 替代普通冰箱 → `dedicated wine storage (not regular fridge)`
- 替代饮料冰箱 → `wine not soda / not a beverage cooler`

**埋词策略：**
- Bullet 2 必须写替代方案优势：`Why choose compressor over thermoelectric?`
- 描述中加入对比语义：`Unlike thermoelectric models, our compressor...`
- 后台词：`compressor wine cooler, not thermoelectric, professional wine storage`

## 五、标准维度埋词策略

### 5.1 品类词
- **wine** (25,262/周) — 单独"wine"搜索量最大但泛化，适合后台Search Terms，不适合标题单独使用
- **wine refrigerator** + **wine cooler** + **wine fridge** — 标题铁三角
- **wine chiller** — 可作为变体标题词，强调快速冷却

### 5.2 安装类型
- freestanding > built-in > under-counter — 搜索量递减
- under cabinet fridge / under cabinet refrigerator — 台面下安装（约212/周）
- bar cabinet with wine fridge — 场景+安装组合词
- table top cooler / tabletop cooler — 桌面型（约620/周）

### 5.3 容量规格
- 166 bottle / 52 bottle / 24 bottle — 主流三档
- 88L — 雪茄柜专用容量标识
- 标题中必须突出容量数字，这是酒柜最核心的决策参数

### 5.4 温区
- dual zone (777+555+186+141=1,659/周) — 双温区是高端标志
- single zone — 入门款
- **建议**：如果产品是双温区，标题必须前置 `dual zone`

### 5.5 制冷技术
- compressor vs thermoelectric — 压缩机是主流，半导体是低端
- 描述中必须声明 compressor cooling，建立技术信任

### 5.6 材质
- stainless steel + glass door — 经典外观组合
- wooden shelves / Spanish cedar — 高端/雪茄柜
- UV protection glass — 专业储酒必备

### 5.7 功能
- temperature control / digital thermostat — 核心功能
- LED light / interior lighting — 展示功能
- lock / lockable — 安全功能（有小孩家庭需求）
- humidity control — 雪茄柜专属
- reversible door — 安装灵活性
- frost free — 免维护

### 5.8 场景
- home bar / kitchen / office / living room / bedroom — 室内场景全覆盖
- RV / camper — 车载场景（小容量需求）
- gift / gifts for men — 礼品场景（搜索量可观）

### 5.9 品牌竞争
**被搜索的品牌：**
- Whynter (166瓶大容量标杆)
- Tylza (88L雪茄柜标杆)

**策略：**广告否定词必须覆盖竞品品牌名（除非打品牌对比广告）

### Point 2 — 替代防御（vs 半导体/冰桶）
> **COMPRESSOR COOLING, NOT THERMoelectric** — Powerful compressor delivers faster, more stable cooling than outdated thermoelectric models. No ice needed, no soggy labels, no temperature swings.

### Point 3 — 核心功能规格

### Point 4 — 材质+保护
> **UV-PROTECTION GLASS + STAINLESS STEEL** — Double-pane tempered glass blocks 90%+ UV rays. Fingerprint-resistant stainless steel frame blends into any kitchen or bar decor.

### Point 5 — 便利+信任
> **WHISPER-QUIET + REVERSIBLE DOOR** — Runs at <45dB— quieter than a normal conversation. Reversible door hinge fits left or right openings. Backed by 24/7 support and 12-month warranty.
